About Us

Pauri Garhwal Swayam Sahaytarth Co-operative (U) Thrift & Credit Society Limited (PGSS), is a cooperative society with its operations rooted in Delhi. Our journey commenced on the 20th of March, 2020, owing to the relentless efforts of Sh. Gabar Singh Gusain, our esteemed Chief Promoter, and Sh. Vikas Singh. PGSS is duly registered under the Delhi Cooperative Societies (DCS) Act of 2003, bearing registration number 10681/Sec-06/TC/2020.

At PGSS, our core mission is to elevate the social and economic well-being of our esteemed members by cultivating a culture of savings. We extend a diverse range of financial products, meticulously tailored to meet the distinct financial aspirations of our members. Our offerings encompass Daily Deposits, Fixed Deposits, Recurring Deposits, Savings Accounts, and Loans.

Nestled in the vibrant landscape of South Delhi, our office serves as the central hub of our operations, guided by the Society's Bye-laws. PGSS ardently adheres to the stringent guidelines of the Delhi State Cooperative Society Act of 2003, conducting business exclusively within our cherished member community. Our financial resources are thoughtfully directed towards member loans and investments, in strict conformity with cooperative society regulations.

Vision

Our vision is both straightforward and profound: to continuously serve our members, uphold a leading position, and guarantee the complete satisfaction of our esteemed customers. We maintain vigilant watchfulness over market dynamics, unwavering in our pursuit of self-improvement, and remain steadfast in our commitment to the cherished principles we hold dear.

Mission

At PGSS, our mission is to pioneer the cooperative movement, establishing the highest standards of quality and prioritizing a customer-centric approach as our guiding principles. Our dedication revolves around serving the future of India through the delivery of exceptional services. We pledge our commitment to fostering a cooperative ethos that not only enriches the lives of our members but also contributes to the prosperity of our great nation.

Membership

Who can be a member of the society?

Any person who is 18 years of age or older, possesses the legal capacity to enter into a valid contract, and is a resident of the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi is eligible to become a member of the Society.

How to become a member of the society?

The application for membership of the society shall be submitted by the applicant at the office of the society, in the prescribed form and KYC documents such as:

  • Id Proof (any one like Voter Id card, Aadhar Card, Pan Card, Driving License etc.)
  • Address Proof (Voter ID card, Electricity/Water Bill, Compulsory)
  • Rent Agreement (In case of tenant. Electricity/Water bill of Landlord compulsory)
  • Latest passport size Photos (2)
  • One Introducer (who is member of society and not a defaulter)

Download Membership Form

Benefits of joining PGSS?

  • Offers higher Interest rates on deposits
  • No TDS is deducted on the Interest earned on the deposits
  • Hassle Free Loan Products
  • Loan at simple interest rates
  • Easy EMIs (Choose EMI amount as per your repaying capacity)
  • No hidden charges
  • Full transparency
